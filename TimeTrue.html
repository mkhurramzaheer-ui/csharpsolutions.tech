Got questions?

Is TimeTrue free? +
There is a free tier for core use. Some advanced capabilities (for example certain reminder or subscription options) may require a paid plan depending on how you configure your account. Check inside the extension for current plan details.
Do I need an account? +
You can explore hover conversion without signing in for many flows. Creating an account unlocks reminders, sync across devices, and Google sign-in where enabled.
Which websites does it work on? +
Normal websites you browse over HTTPS. Some special Chrome pages and a few heavily sandboxed surfaces may block extensions — that's expected browser behavior.
Will it slow down my browser? +
TimeTrue is designed to be lightweight. Work happens as pages load and when you interact; you should not notice meaningful slowdown on typical hardware.
Does it handle daylight saving time? +
Yes — conversions use your browser's timezone data, which includes DST rules for IANA regions.
What data does TimeTrue collect? +
The extension reads visible page text to find times. If you create an account, we store profile and authentication data and reminder content needed for sync and notifications, processed through our backend (Supabase) and payment providers where applicable. We do not sell your personal data. Read the full privacy policy for categories and choices.
Can I set my timezone manually? +
Yes — TimeTrue uses your system timezone by default, and you can adjust preferences in the extension settings, including pinned cities where supported.
