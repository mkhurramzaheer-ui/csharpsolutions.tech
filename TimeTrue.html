Got questions?

Is TimeTrue really free? +
Yes — completely free. No subscription, no trial, no hidden fees. Install and use it forever at no cost.
Which websites does it work on? +
Every website. Gmail, Outlook Web, Twitter/X, Reddit, Slack Web, Notion, LinkedIn, news sites, forums — if there's a time written on the page, TimeTrue finds it.
Will it slow down my browser? +
Not noticeably. TimeTrue is lightweight by design — it scans page text only when a page loads and uses minimal memory. You won't feel it running.
Does it handle daylight saving time? +
Yes, automatically. TimeTrue accounts for daylight saving changes for every timezone — so you always get the correct local time, never an off-by-one-hour mistake.
What data does it collect? +
Nothing. All conversions happen locally inside your browser. No data is ever sent anywhere. No account required. Your privacy is completely protected.
Can I set my timezone manually? +
Yes. TimeTrue detects your timezone automatically but you can override it anytime from the settings. You can also pin up to 5 additional cities to always show alongside your local time.
