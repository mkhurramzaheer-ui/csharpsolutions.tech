Privacy Policy for TimeTrue

Product: TimeTrue – Time Zone Converter & Meeting Reminders (Chrome extension)
Effective date: June 1, 2026
Last updated: June 1, 2026

TimeTrue (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a browser extension that converts times shown on webpages, lets you pin cities and set reminders, and optionally syncs settings and reminders when you sign in. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Summary: Time detection runs in your browser. We do not sell your data, use it for ads, or track you across websites. We do not collect payment card information. We do not collect GPS or physical location.

1) Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

Account and profile information

When you create an account or sign in (email/password or Google), we collect your email address and basic profile information such as your display name.

Authentication information

Session and authentication tokens required to keep you signed in securely and to sync your data when you use a signed-in account.

Time zone settings

Time zone names you choose in the extension (for example America/New_York, Europe/London) and your device’s time zone from the browser clock (IANA time zone identifier only). These are used for conversion and reminders.

We do not collect GPS coordinates, street address, or IP-based location tracking.

Webpage text for time detection (local processing)

TimeTrue analyzes visible text on webpages in your browser to detect and convert time expressions when you hover or use conversion features. We do not upload full webpage content to our servers for this purpose.

Detected page text is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for cross-site tracking.

Reminders and settings

Reminders you create (event name, message, scheduled time), pinned cities, notification preferences, and related settings. If you are signed in, this data may be stored on our backend to sync across your devices.

Local device storage

Some settings and reminders may be stored locally on your device (browser storage) to improve speed and reliability, including when you are not signed in.

2) How we use data

We use data only to:

  • Provide time zone conversion on webpages
  • Show local and pinned-city times
  • Create and deliver reminder notifications (desktop and, when enabled, email)
  • Sync reminders and settings for signed-in users
  • Maintain account security and prevent abuse
  • Send transactional emails (such as account verification) when applicable

We do not use your data for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

3) Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We use service providers strictly needed to operate TimeTrue:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend services
  • Resend — transactional email (for example account verification and reminder emails, when enabled)
  • Google — optional Google sign-in (OAuth), subject to Google’s privacy terms when you choose that option

These providers process data on our behalf according to their security practices and terms.

4) Local processing

Time detection and conversion on webpages happen primarily in your browser. Only account data, reminders, settings, and data required to run signed-in features are sent to our backend.

5) Browser permissions

TimeTrue requests permissions only for extension functionality:

  • storage — save settings, reminders, and session state
  • notifications and alarms — deliver reminder notifications
  • identity — secure sign-in, including Google sign-in
  • tabs — open or focus extension pages
  • scripting and host access — detect and convert visible time text on webpages you visit

6) Data retention

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Contact).

7) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user data. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

8) Children’s privacy

TimeTrue is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9) Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Remove the extension at any time
  • Change settings in the extension
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of account data by emailing us

10) International users

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable law and our service providers’ safeguards.

11) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date at the top when we do. Continued use of TimeTrue after changes means you accept the updated policy.

If we later enable paid subscriptions, we will update this policy before collecting payment-related information and will process payments only through a certified payment provider (not stored in the extension).
