Privacy Policy for TimeTrue – Instant Timezone Converter

Effective date: May 7, 2026

TimeTrue ("we", "our", "us") provides a browser extension that converts times shown on webpages, allows users to set reminders, and optionally syncs settings and reminders across devices.

1) Data We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

Account and profile information

Email address and basic profile/account data when you sign up or sign in.

Authentication information

Session and authentication tokens required to securely keep you signed in.

Timezone and region preferences

Timezone and region-related preferences used for time conversion and reminders.

We do not collect precise GPS location data for tracking purposes.

Website content required for core functionality

TimeTrue temporarily analyzes visible webpage text locally within your browser to detect and convert time expressions.

Webpage content is not sold, shared for advertising purposes, or used for cross-site tracking.

Reminder and settings data

Reminders you create, app settings, favorite cities, and related preference data.

Local device storage

Some reminder and settings data may be stored locally on your device to improve performance, speed, and reminder reliability.

Billing and subscription data (if applicable)

Subscription status and limited billing-related metadata provided by payment processors.

We do not store full card numbers or card security codes.

2) How We Use Data

We use data to:

  • Provide timezone conversion features on webpages
  • Detect and display local times
  • Create and deliver reminder notifications
  • Sync reminders and settings across signed-in devices
  • Maintain account security and prevent abuse
  • Provide subscription and premium features (if enabled)

3) Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or use personal data for advertising or tracking purposes.

We may process or share limited data with service providers strictly required to operate the service, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend services
  • Stripe — subscription and billing workflows (if enabled)

These providers process data according to their own security practices and privacy terms.

4) Local Processing

TimeTrue performs timezone detection and conversion primarily within the user’s browser.

Only account, reminder, synchronization, and required service-related data is transmitted to backend services.

5) Permissions Use

TimeTrue requests browser permissions only for extension functionality:

  • storage — save settings, reminders, and session state
  • notifications and alarms — deliver reminder notifications
  • identity — secure sign-in, including Google sign-in
  • tabs — open or focus extension pages and sync settings updates
  • host permissions — detect and convert visible time text on webpages

Google account authentication is used only for secure sign-in and account synchronization.

6) Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the service
  • Maintain account functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce agreements

Users may request account and reminder data deletion by contacting support.

7) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to help protect user data.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

8) Children’s Privacy

TimeTrue is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9) Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Remove the extension at any time
  • Access or update your settings
  • Request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting us

10) International Users

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards and service provider controls.

11) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, we will update the “Effective date” above.
